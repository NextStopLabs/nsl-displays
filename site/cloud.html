What you run from here

The depot desk for every bus.

Programs, codes & stops

Services with destinations, unique 1–8 digit codes, ordered stops with terminus rules — validated on publish so a bad code can never reach a bus.

Board layout editor

Draw each destination’s boards against a live LED preview with per-element fonts, scaling, spacing, scrolling and rotating board pages.

Audio & voices

Upload clips or generate speech with on-board voices, trim announcements from the library, and assign next, approaching and terminus audio per service.

Pairing & rollout

Pair each bus with a one-time code, publish a program version, and watch controllers pick it up — audio and software updates travel the same way.

Remote control

See what each bus is showing, which stop it’s at, and drive it from the desk: codes, stops, announcements, overrides, volume and programs.

Fleet software updates

Stage signed software releases per channel and let buses install them on their next heartbeat — with automatic rollback on failure.

Your data, your organisation

Multi-user, scoped by organisation.

Sign up, get your organisation, invite owners, editors and viewers. Every program, clip, controller and release belongs to exactly one organisation — the bus only ever sees its own.
