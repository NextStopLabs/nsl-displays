How it works

Design once. Every bus follows.

Build in the cloud

Services, destinations, 1–8 digit codes, stops, announcements and board layouts — designed with a live LED preview and published as one program file.

Sync over hotspot

The bus pulls its program, audio and software the next time it sees the internet. Afterwards it needs nothing — the depot Wi-Fi, a phone hotspot, any connection will do.

Depart with the boards

The driver keys a code, the front, side and rear signs follow together. Drop the signal and the signs hold their last frame instead of going dark.

Why operators choose NSL

Built for the bus, not the demo room.

Offline-first

The bus runs entirely from local cache. Internet is only needed to sync something new.

Real Hanover glyphs

Boards render true 1:1 Hanover bitmap letterforms — the same shapes drivers and passengers already read.

Hold last frame

Side and rear signs keep showing their board if the bus LAN drops, instead of blanking mid-service.
