The set

Three sizes, one program.

Front · 240×40Driven direct off the front Pi over HUB75. Route number left, destination and via right — or any layout you design per destination.
Side · 160×40Driven over the bus LAN by its own receiver Pi. Shows the same destination in its own layout, sized for the kerb.
Rear · 80×40Driven over the bus LAN by its own receiver Pi. Route number behind you, all the way home.
Hold last frameIf the bus LAN drops, side and rear signs keep showing their board instead of going dark.

Layouts

Every destination, dressed for its route.

Board pages that rotate

A destination can carry several board pages — each with its own via and dest — that take turns on the signs every few seconds. One page in the program, one board at a time on the street.

Type, tuned per element

Every element sets its own Hanover font, integer scale for tall boards, letter spacing, alignment and scrolling. Long names scroll; short ones sit still.

Insert pages

Need an extra board after the booked ones — a diversion, a terminating short? The driver inserts a destination page that rides along until cleared.

Designed in the cloud

Layouts are drawn in the portal against a live LED preview — what you see is what the bus shows, pixel for pixel.
