In the cab

Built for gloves and glare.

Destination codesType a 1–8 digit code, or browse services and destinations with the arrow keys. The boards follow instantly.
Departure timerPress F, key the departure time — the boards lock on and count down “Departing in X mins”, switching to live seconds for the final 30.
Insert pagesSlot an extra destination into the rotation for diversions and shorts; clear it with one key.
OverridesPush a manual message to the boards — held, or full-screen — cleared the moment it’s served its purpose.
AnnouncementsNext stop, approaching and terminus clips at the driver’s command, through the bus PA.
Wired to the busGPIO rules react to doors, reverse gear and toggle switches — boards and audio follow the vehicle, not just the keypad.

Offline by design

No signal, no problem.

The controller hosts its own control page on the bus network and runs everything from local cache. Pairing, programs and updates arrive from the cloud when there’s a connection — the service never waits for one.
