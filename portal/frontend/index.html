GPIO pins - free vs reserved (Raspberry Pi 4B, 40-pin header)

            
Grey pins are taken by the LED matrix (HUB75) and can't be used for buttons. Green pins are free GPIOs. Black/red are power/ground, brown is the EEPROM ID pair - none of those are usable either. Pick a controller to use its matrix mapping; otherwise the default (regular, parallel 1) is shown.
